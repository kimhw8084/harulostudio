# Cobalt Ember production system

Harulo has one public identity. There is no production palette selector and no
candidate logo archive.

The canonical mark is generated from `lib/brand/geometry.ts` and rendered by
`components/brand/harulo-mark.tsx`. Cobalt is the structural color, Ember is
the active signal, and the two tiers create publication rhythm. The mark is
used in navigation, the Publication Genesis hero, showcase editions, press
assets, favicons, metadata artwork and the final footer return.

Showcase concepts are deliberately separate from verified product data. They
are useful for testing the visual system, but do not imply availability.
